Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does the smart neck belt battery last? ▼
The DairyPulse smart belt is engineered with custom ultra-low-power Sub-GHz LoRaWAN telemetry and an industrial lithium-thionyl chloride cell that lasts up to 3 years under standard 15-minute telemetry intervals. The Farm Control Center will alert you when a battery drops below 20%.
Is the collar completely waterproof when buffaloes submerge in water ponds? ▼
Yes! The hardware capsule is rated IP67 water and mud resistant. Water buffaloes can wallow in mud or submerge in ponds without damaging the internal sensors or RF antenna.
How accurate is the silent heat (estrus) detection in water buffaloes? ▼
Water buffaloes frequently exhibit 'silent heat' during nocturnal hours (1:00 AM - 5:00 AM) with minimal physical discharge. DairyPulse's dual sensor fusion (rumination drop + micro-temperature elevation + pacing accelerometry) detects buffalo estrus with over 90% confidence, sending an alert directly to your phone.
How far can the cows graze from the LoRa gateway antenna? ▼
The DairyPulse LoRa gateway provides a coverage radius of up to 5 kilometers in open rural terrain and penetrates through tin sheds, concrete feed bunks, and tree cover.
